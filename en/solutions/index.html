Solutions

Pogon MES and connected shop-floor applications

Pogon.io connects production, quality, planning, management and business systems through a controlled data flow.

01

Work order management

Order selection, operator login, workstations, start and finish flow, planned and produced quantities.

02

Operator screens

Simple interfaces with large controls, built for industrial environments and different workstation types.

03

Scrap and quality

Scrap type, quantity, reason, time and connection to operator, item and work order.

04

Downtime and breaks

Start, finish, reason and duration of downtime, including breaks, tool changes and loss analysis.

05

Dashboards and KPI

TV dashboards, management views, active orders, trends and filtering by period, line, item or station.

06

Administration

Users, roles, items, standards, settings, workstations, shift handover and permissions.

Integrations

Connection with the existing information environment

Integration may include ERP systems, SAP environments, internal databases, APIs, quality systems, reporting tools and industrial devices when technically feasible.

Integration options depend on the client’s existing information system, available interfaces and technical conditions.

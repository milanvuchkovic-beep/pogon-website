How We Work

Implementation is gradual, controlled and designed to reduce operational risk

The process first understands production, then validates a pilot scope before expanding the solution.

01

Analysis

Client discussions, process walk-throughs, data-flow mapping, document review and ERP overview.

02

Pilot scope

Selection of one line, station or process, expected outcomes and success criteria.

03

Configuration and development

Roles, items, workflows, screens, integrations and reports are adapted to the client context.

04

Testing

Local tests, test data, controlled environment, user testing, stability and security checks.

05

Pilot implementation

Limited users and stations, behavior monitoring and controlled corrections.

06

Rollout and support

Training, instructions, technical support, monitoring and development of future phases.

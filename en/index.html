How the system works

From order to result

Each production step leaves a clear, connected record — from order launch to final reporting.

  1. Work order

    Planned work enters production with item, quantity and priority clearly defined.

  2. Operator and station

    The operator sees the order, starts work and records data where production happens.

  3. Production and quantities

    Produced quantities and statuses are tracked through one connected production record.

  4. Quality and scrap

    Checks, deviations and scrap are tied to the order, item and point of origin.

  5. Downtime and breaks

    Losses get a reason, duration and connection to a specific machine or station.

  6. Dashboards and reports

    Management gets production visibility without manual data consolidation.
